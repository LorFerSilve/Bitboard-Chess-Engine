## Decisions and limitations

Screening candidates for independent validation: **King Pressure**, **Knight Forks**, optionally **Clustered TT** and **Adaptive Aspiration**. The rest have no convincing positive signal; **Pawn Support** is especially concerning. The estimated Elo gains are exploratory; none of these 120-game individual tests on its own establishes a reliable superiority claim. Multiple comparisons raise false-positive risk. All tests use the same 95-opening EPD collection, albeit a different shuffle seed from earlier runs.

The control was close to the historical v0.5 under these test conditions, but its near-zero difference does not establish full parity for every position and time control.

Next: evaluate combined candidates against historical v0.5 with seed `20261102` (200 games each) in [combination-selection workflow](https://github.com/LorFerSilve/Bitboard-Chess-Engine/blob/feat/v0.6-search-eval/.github/workflows/v06-combinations.yml), then confirm the selected release configuration on independently drawn openings and longer time controls.
