## Selection decision

The **Clustered TT + Adaptive Aspiration** pair is the only positive point estimate in this five-way comparison and is selected **only for independent confirmation**, not for release. Its 95% interval as reported by Fastchess spans zero. Earlier seeds also gave weak or negative TT outcomes; the chosen pair could simply be a winner selected by chance from several tries. Null Verification does not improve this combination on this seed and will not be included in the confirmation candidate.

Next, use [the dedicated 400-game confirmation workflow](https://github.com/LorFerSilve/Bitboard-Chess-Engine/blob/feat/v0.6-search-eval/.github/workflows/v06-confirm-tt-aspiration.yml), new opening shuffle seed `20261105`, 200 paired rounds at 3+0.03, the unchanged v0.5 binary, and only `All Heuristics=false`, `Clustered TT=true`, `Adaptive Aspiration=true`. This is a **fresh confirmation set**, not a reason to add estimates from previously screened variants uncritically. Note that a new shuffle of the same 95-position book is **not** an independent opening corpus; follow up with a more diverse book and longer time control before any general strength/release claim.

If the confirmation does not establish a convincing advantage, leave v0.5 the strongest validated release and keep v0.6 experimental. NNUE and multi-threaded search remain deferred.
