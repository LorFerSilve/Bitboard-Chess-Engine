## Interpretation and limitations

v0.5 shows a clearly positive head-to-head strength gain under **these exact experimental conditions**. This is a **relative Elo difference**, not an absolute chess rating. The match used the repository's 95-position EPD opening suite (some openings may recur), one CPU environment and one fast time control. A second match with a larger, independent opening book and longer time controls is recommended before extrapolating the magnitude of this improvement broadly.

This experiment tested the entire v0.5 release against v0.4, not individual search changes in isolation; the Elo increase cannot be attributed to one optimization without ablation matches.
