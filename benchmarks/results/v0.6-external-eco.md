## Interpretation and release decision

1. **No configuration passed a positive-Elo confidence threshold.** Every reported Elo interval includes zero. In particular, QSearch SEE's +24.36 estimate has an approximate interval of −2.50 to +51.22 Elo, *before accounting for multiple comparisons*.
2. **QSearch SEE Pruning is the only positive candidate worth follow-up**, with signs consistent across its direct v0.5 and isolated all-off comparisons, but it remains unproven. Do not combine or subtract estimates across different matches as though they were a single paired experiment.
3. The Clustered TT + Adaptive Aspiration and Null Verification combinations did not replicate earlier favorable screening signals; deprioritize rather than enable them by default. The all-off control is essentially level with v0.5 within uncertainty.
4. Fastchess emitted repeated `PV continues after threefold repetition` warnings from **both** engines. These concern reported principal variations and warrant a separate PV/repetition investigation; they do *not*, by themselves, prove illegal played moves or invalidate finished games. The five match jobs finished successfully.
5. **Keep historical v0.5 as the strongest validated release.** Keep all eleven experimental v0.6 heuristics *off* by default and do not merge PR #2 based on this screen.
