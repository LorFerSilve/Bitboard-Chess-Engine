## Existing 200-game checks against unchanged historical v0.5

The already-completed [200-game king/fork/TT combination test](v0.6-combinations.md) and [fork/TT selection](https://github.com/LorFerSilve/Bitboard-Chess-Engine/actions/runs/41305631097) show:

- Clustered TT alone: **+6.95 ±34.14 Elo**.
- Knight Forks alone: **−6.95 ±34.49 Elo**.
- Knight Forks + Clustered TT: **−29.60 ±33.02 Elo**.
- All-off v0.6 control versus v0.5: **−22.62 ±35.55 Elo** on that seed.

The favorable clustered-TT point estimate remains smaller than the uncertainty. No release claim is justified. Positive results found by searching many variants over related opening suites require independent confirmation.
